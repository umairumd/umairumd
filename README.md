### what i've built

**[AgencyOS](https://github.com/umairumd/agencyos)** — Internal operations platform for a digital agency. Multi-workspace RBAC, async job engine, AI-powered proposals via Gemini, Google Drive and Notion integrations, invoice management, and encrypted credential vault.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Gemini AI` `Vercel` `Hetzner`

---

**[UniMate](https://github.com/umairumd/unimate)** — University timetable management system. Excel upload pipeline parses teacher, class, and room data into Firestore then pushes FCM notifications to students before each class. Flutter mobile app, React admin panel, Firebase Cloud Functions backend.

`Flutter` `React` `Firebase` `Cloud Functions` `TypeScript` `SQLite`

---

**[EdgePress](https://github.com/umairumd/edgepress)** — Production agency website with headless WordPress CMS. Portfolio images sliced server-side with Sharp and streamed progressively to the client. Full Yoast SEO pipeline into Next.js metadata. WordPress-driven redirects at the Edge.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `WordPress` `WPGraphQL` `Sharp` `Vercel`

---

**[Dossier](https://github.com/umairumd/dossier)** — Internal HR platform for team operations. Daily progress reports, manager review dashboard, leave and attendance tracking, in-app notifications, and contract management. Multi-tenant RBAC with invite flow.

`Next.js 16` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `TypeScript` `Tailwind` `Resend`

---

**[WaltonAI](https://github.com/umairumd/walton-ai)** — Multilingual WhatsApp AI commerce system. Handles product discovery, cart management, PDF quotations, order confirmation, and automatic Shopify sync. Responds in English, Roman Urdu, and Urdu.

`n8n` `Gemini 2.5 Flash` `WAHA` `Supabase` `Shopify` `Hetzner`

---

**[HireScout](https://github.com/umairumd/hirescout)** — AI-powered hiring pipeline. Applicants submit via Google Forms, responses route through Gemini for evaluation, structured candidate profiles land in Notion automatically. Self-hosted on Hetzner.

`n8n` `Gemini AI` `Notion` `Google Sheets` `Hetzner`
